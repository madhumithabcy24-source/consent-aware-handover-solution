Demonstration guide for viva, evaluation, and operational workflow.

📞 Voice Contact Representation Note

In a real-world youth helpline, client interactions occur via both text chat and voice phone calls. In this MVP, voice contacts are represented as synthetic transcribed text (prefixed with [Voice Transcript]). The preprocessing and NLP pipeline ingests these transcripts identically to text chat logs.

2. Key Features & Demonstration Steps

A. Viewing Handover Summaries

Navigate to Sessions and click View Handover on any record. The system runs ML relevance, DL sensitivity detection, consent verification, and role filtering to generate a safe continuity summary.

B. Real-Time Consent Revocation

Navigate to Manage Consent for any session (or click Edit / Revoke Consent on the handover page). Toggle Sensitive Notes Consent to No and save. Upon viewing the next summary, the sensitive content immediately updates to display 🔒 Restricted by consent.

C. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Log in as socialworker / 1234. Observe that sensitive clinical disclosures remain restricted with 🔒 Restricted by role by default, even if the client granted consent, because Social Worker scope is restricted to non-clinical social care handovers.

D. Low-Confidence Human Review Flag

When the ML relevance or DL sensitivity prediction confidence falls below 70%, the system displays a prominent ⚠️ Low Confidence Warning Banner notifying the worker that human review is recommended before taking clinical action.

E. Baseline vs. Proposed System Comparison

Navigate to Baseline vs Proposed to compare the raw un-filtered baseline handover against the safe proposed system side-by-side. The interactive Chart.js bar chart illustrates reductions in consent violations and sensitive content exposure.
